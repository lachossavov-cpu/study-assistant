STUDY ASSISTANT

Your deadlines, read out loud.

A dashboard that follows your Brightspace courses and Teams timetable, tells you what is due, and speaks it. Everything runs on your own computer — nothing is uploaded anywhere.

1 · Download

⊞ Windows Unzip, double-click Assistant.exe. Nothing to install. Mac Unzip, double-click Start.command. Needs Python 3.10+.
Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" — the app is not signed yet. Click More info → Run anyway.

2 · First run

  1. Give your assistant a name.
  2. Press Open sign-in window and log in to Brightspace with your school account. Your password goes to your school's own page — the app never sees it.
  3. Press Find my courses and tick the ones to follow.
  4. Optional: switch on Teams to see your class timetable, and phone access to open the same dashboard on your phone.

3 · Add it to your phone

  1. In Settings (⚙) on the computer, turn on Phone access and restart the app.
  2. On your phone, on the same Wi-Fi, open the address the app shows and enter the six-digit code.
  3. Add it to your home screen: iPhone — Share → Add to Home Screen; Android — ⋮ → Add to Home screen.
The computer has to be on and running the app. School Wi-Fi such as eduroam usually blocks this; home Wi-Fi works.

Updates

You download it once. When the app starts it checks this project for a newer version, fetches it, and uses it the next time you open the app — and tells you what changed. If an update ever fails to start, the app goes back to the version before it by itself.

What stays private

  • Your data stays on your computer. Your tasks, your course list, your timetable and your notes are not uploaded.
  • Assignment documents are the exception. To write the summary and steps under an assignment, that assignment's documents from Brightspace are sent to the app's summary service and to Anthropic (Claude). Nothing about you goes with them, and each assignment is summarised once and shared with classmates in the same course.
  • You sign in yourself, on Brightspace's and Microsoft's own pages. The app never stores a password.
  • Teams is read-only. It reads your timetable; it never sends messages, mail or changes anything.
  • The text being read aloud goes to Microsoft's voice service to be turned into speech.
